Rules for sampling configurations

100 particles, 300 quanta. Two distributions and two ways to sample them.

Energy transferIndependent configuration
Boltzmann*Choose a particle and a destination at random.
Transfer ε if the source is occupied.		Randomly draw an occupation vector.
All allowed vectors have equal probability.
BinomialChoose a packet and a destination at random.
Transfer the packet.		Assign each packet independently
to one of the 100 particles at random.

* The Boltzmann distribution is an approximation for this finite system.
