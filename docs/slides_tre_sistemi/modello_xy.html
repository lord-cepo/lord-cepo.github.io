XY model: continuous spins and vortices

Checking for WebGPU…

1600 × 900 lattice. Each cell is coloured by its spin orientation. Interpolation smooths the displayed pixels.

The wheel divides orientations into six 60° sectors. The spin angles themselves vary continuously.

Each site on the periodic lattice holds a spin with a continuous angle. Neighbouring spins lower their energy by aligning.

H=−J∑⟨i,j⟩cos(θi−θj)

Metropolis sampling proposes a rotation of a randomly chosen spin. It accepts the rotation with probability min(1, exp(−ΔH/kBT)). On the GPU, a sweep proposes one rotation per site in two checkerboard passes. On the CPU, a sweep contains one proposal per site on average.

To detect a vortex, traverse the four sides of a plaquette counterclockwise and sum the angle differences wrapped into the range −π to π. A winding of +2π marks a vortex, and −2π marks an antivortex.

At low temperature thermal vortices are rare and tend to remain bound to antivortices. Heating can unbind these pairs and increase the number of defects. This is the mechanism of the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition. Kosterlitz and Thouless
