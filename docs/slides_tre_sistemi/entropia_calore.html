Chapter 4 · Direction of energy exchange

Heat flows in the direction that increases entropy

Total entropy increases when heat flows from hot to cold.

Total energy is conserved

Total entropy increases

From the definition of temperature at fixed N and V:

1T=∂S∂E⇒δS=δET

δScaldo=−δETcaldoδSfreddo=δETfreddo
δStot=δScaldo+δSfreddo=δE(1Tfreddo−1Tcaldo)≥0

Higher entropy means more configurations, so this direction is statistically favoured.
