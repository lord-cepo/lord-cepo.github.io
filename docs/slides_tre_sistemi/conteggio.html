Chapter 4 · Counting states

From stars and bars to Boltzmann

N distinguishable particles and Q indistinguishable quanta. Assume the vectors (n₁, …, nN) whose entries sum to Q are equally likely.

Counting all configurations

Q stars represent the quanta. N − 1 dividers distribute them among the particles, allowing some particles to have zero energy.
