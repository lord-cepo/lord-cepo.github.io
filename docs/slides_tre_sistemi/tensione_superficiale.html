Surface tension and soap bubbles

The cost of creating an interface

Surface tension γ is the free-energy cost per unit increase in interfacial area A at fixed temperature. In a lattice gas, a particle at the boundary has fewer occupied neighbours and loses attractive bonds. The interface can also take many shapes, which contribute to entropy.

The smaller bubble has higher pressure

A soap bubble has two air–liquid interfaces. For a thin film of radius R, the surface free energy is ℱfilm = 2γ · 4πR². Work done by the excess pressure balances the cost of expansion.
