When is particle insertion favourable?

Propose filling the central site. Each occupied neighbour adds a bond of energy −ε. At μ/ε = −2, the chemical potential term costs +2ε.

ΔK=Δ(H−μN)=−nε−μ=(2−n)ε

Blue marks an occupied neighbour and grey an empty one. The + marks the insertion site. Only the four edge-sharing neighbours count.

0 occupied neighbours

+

ΔK = +2ε

Pacc = exp(−2ε / kBT)

In a dilute gas with no occupied neighbours, no bond forms and insertion costs energy.

2 occupied neighbours

+

ΔK = 0

Pacc = 1

The two bonds exactly offset the chemical potential cost.

3 occupied neighbours

+

ΔK = −ε

Pacc = 1

The three bonds make insertion energetically favourable.
