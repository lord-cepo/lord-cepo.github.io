CHAPTER 5 · SLIDE 7

Lattice Boltzmann in two dimensions: D2Q9

160 × 64 nodes. A uniform force drives the fluid to the right. Horizontal boundaries are periodic, and populations bounce back at the walls and obstacle.

The fluid starts at rest. The wake evolves over thousands of steps and re-enters from the left through the periodic boundary. The slider changes viscosity at fixed temperature. Lowering τ reduces viscosity and can slow relaxation. Contrast is fixed at 2× and each update advances 30 steps. Numerical stability becomes more sensitive near τ = 0.5. This is a single-phase model.

Each node holds nine populations: one at rest, four axial and four diagonal.
