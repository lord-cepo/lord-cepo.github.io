From chapter 1 to chapter 2

Representing collisions as energy exchange

We look for statistical properties without reconstructing every trajectory.

Tracking every collision is difficult. We replace it with the exchange of an energy quantum between model particles. The dynamics differ from the real system, but we hope to recover its statistical properties. In chapters 3 and 4, we will sample configurations and then count them.

Mechanical collisions and exchange of one quantum

Energy conservation alone is insufficient. The constraints and sampling probabilities determine which statistical properties the model reproduces.
