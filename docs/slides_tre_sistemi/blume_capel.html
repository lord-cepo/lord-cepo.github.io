Magnetization m measures the imbalance between +1 and −1. Magnetic density ρ measures the fraction of nonzero sites. Two opposing domains can give m ≈ 0 even when ρ is large.

Each site has three states, −1, 0 and +1. Nonzero spins lower the energy when neighbouring spins have the same sign. Positive D favours state 0.

The term sᵢ² equals 1 for ±1 and 0 for state 0. D therefore sets the energy cost of a magnetic site, analogous to an occupation cost in a lattice gas.

Order and the tricritical point

At low temperature, raise D/J from 1.8 to 2.3. Anisotropy becomes strong enough to favour sites in state 0 over aligned domains.

On an infinite square lattice at zero field, the continuous transition line meets the first-order line near D/J = 1,966 and kBT/J = 0,608. Monte Carlo study

128 × 128 periodic lattice. Metropolis sampling chooses a site and proposes either of the other two states with equal probability. Each sweep includes N proposals. Near the transition, instantaneous results depend on the initial state and relaxation.
