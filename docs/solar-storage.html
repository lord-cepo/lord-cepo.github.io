Solar + battery storage

How much gross margin can a solar plant earn when a battery lets it shift electricity sales across hours and participate in balancing markets?

The project

I’m developing a simulation of a photovoltaic plant paired with a battery energy storage system (BESS). The aim is to estimate the gross margin from selling electricity in Italy’s day-ahead market (Mercato del Giorno Prima, MGP) and participating in balancing markets.

What the simulation will compare

Solar only

Use sales of photovoltaic generation in the day-ahead market as a baseline.

Solar + storage on MGP

Estimate the value of storing solar output and selling it later, accounting for the battery’s power, energy capacity and round-trip losses.

Solar + storage with balancing

Explore how reserving battery capacity for balancing changes the gross margin relative to day-ahead trading alone.
