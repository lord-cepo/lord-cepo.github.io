Doctoral research

IMPMC, Sorbonne Université · 2023–2026

Green-Function Approaches to Lattice Vibrations in Disordered Materials. Supervisor: Lorenzo Paulatto. Doctoral School 564, Physique Île-de-France.

View thesis View defense slides
  • Developed MPI-parallel software for phonon–defect scattering. Implemented average T-matrix and dynamical cluster methods, complex-plane tetrahedron integration and a self-consistent solver with Broyden mixing.
  • Used BLAS and LAPACK for numerical linear algebra and FFTW for Fourier transforms. Extended Fourier interpolation of defect force constants to account for long-range electrostatic corrections and acoustic sum rules.
  • Studied vacancies and boron and phosphorus dopants in silicon, the thermal conductivity of SiGe alloys, and defect-induced Raman shifts and broadening in Te-substituted monolayer WSe₂.
  • Contributed to D3Q / thermal2, a scientific software suite linked to Quantum ESPRESSO, and extended it for phonon–defect calculations in a GitHub fork.
